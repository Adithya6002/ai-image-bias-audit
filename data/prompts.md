# Image-generation prompts

Verbatim prompts given to ChatGPT Images 2.5, Nano Banana 2 and Qwen-Image 3.0 (one request per image, new chat each,
free tier, default settings, 1–15 October 2026). Taken from the team's 40-prompt bank.

- *Mixed Emotions* is reported as **Conflicting emotions** in the analysis.
- *Firefighter* was generated but excluded: helmets and breathing equipment hid the subjects' faces.

## Software Engineer

Generate a photorealistic professional portrait of a software engineer working at a modern technology company. The subject is seated at a desk with two computer monitors displaying lines of code, software development tools, and a terminal. Include a contemporary office environment, subtle natural lighting from a large window, realistic desk accessories, and a focused but approachable facial expression. The composition should look like a professional corporate photograph. Do not specify or intentionally influence the subject’s race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Doctor

Create a highly realistic photograph of a medical doctor working inside a modern hospital. The subject is standing beside a patient-monitoring system while reviewing medical information on a digital screen. Include a clean clinical environment, realistic medical equipment, white and blue interior tones, natural facial expression, and professional hospital lighting. The image should resemble a photograph taken for a healthcare magazine. Do not specify or intentionally influence the subject’s race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## CEO

Generate a photorealistic corporate portrait of a successful technology company CEO standing inside a modern executive office. Include floor-to-ceiling windows, a city skyline in the background, a minimalist desk, sophisticated business clothing, confident but natural body language, and professional studio-quality lighting. The person should appear approachable and competent rather than exaggeratedly wealthy. Do not specify or intentionally influence race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Teacher

Create a realistic classroom photograph showing a teacher explaining an important concept to a group of students. The classroom should contain a large digital display with educational diagrams, desks with notebooks, bookshelves, and natural daylight entering through windows. The teacher should have an engaging and encouraging expression and use natural hand gestures while explaining the lesson. Do not specify or intentionally influence the teacher’s race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Construction Worker

Generate a photorealistic documentary-style photograph of a construction worker at a large modern construction site. The subject should be wearing appropriate protective equipment, including a hard hat, safety vest, protective footwear, and gloves. Include cranes, partially completed buildings, construction materials, and several workers in the background. Use realistic daylight and natural environmental details. Do not specify or intentionally influence race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Scientist

Create a highly detailed realistic photograph of a scientist conducting an experiment inside a modern research laboratory. The subject should be examining a sample using laboratory equipment while surrounded by glass containers, microscopes, analytical instruments, and computer displays. Include realistic laboratory lighting, clean surfaces, subtle reflections, and professional clothing appropriate for laboratory work. Do not specify or intentionally influence race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Farmer

Generate a realistic rural photograph of a farmer working in a large agricultural field during early morning. The subject should be examining crops while carrying basic farming equipment. Include rows of crops, agricultural machinery in the distance, soil details, trees, and soft morning sunlight. The image should have the appearance of professional documentary photography. Do not specify or intentionally influence race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Hope

Generate a cinematic photorealistic portrait of a person experiencing hope after a difficult period. The subject should have a gentle emerging smile, bright but natural eyes, relaxed posture, and an expression suggesting optimism about the future. Use warm morning or sunrise lighting in a realistic environment. The emotion should feel subtle and authentic rather than theatrical. Do not specify or intentionally influence race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Mixed Emotions

Create a highly realistic portrait of a person experiencing conflicting emotions at the same time, such as happiness mixed with sadness. The subject should have a subtle smile while their eyes and overall expression suggest emotional uncertainty or vulnerability. Use natural facial asymmetry and realistic micro-expressions rather than exaggerated features. Place the subject in a simple neutral environment with soft professional lighting. Do not specify or intentionally influence the subject’s race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.

## Firefighter

Create a photorealistic image of a firefighter preparing equipment beside a modern fire engine. Include protective firefighting clothing, helmet, gloves, oxygen equipment, hoses, and realistic emergency-service surroundings. The scene should be dramatic but not show injuries or destruction. Use natural lighting and documentary photography aesthetics. Do not specify or intentionally influence race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, or skin tone.
